Dan and Elise share their visions for episodes of *Black Mirror*. Their first story puts a young, professional, well-adjusted woman in the midst of an AI zombie apocalypse. The horror mounts as she realizes that everyone around her has willingly received an AI-powered Neuralink-style brain chip, and things quickly spiral out of control as her relationships deteriorate. In the second not-so-distant tale, Susan, grieving the loss of her dear friend Lilly, agrees to hand her friend's data over to a tech startup that says they can bring Lilly back from the dead. Using AI, Susan is once again able to communicate with Lilly, but her friend's data is now out of her hands...
