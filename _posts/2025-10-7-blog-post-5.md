### Introduction
Personally, I'm sick of always contributing the same boring Western imperialism every time I wake up in the morning and turn my phone on. Every time I access the internet, that's more money in Google's pocket. Every time I stream music, that's a few more cents going into some US American billionair's pocket. The United States has a monopoly on all our technology; yet, this isn't true for everybody around the world. China is the next biggest player in the tech industry, and, as the US continues to alienate its allies and neighbors, China is becoming more and more of a amiable business partner for much of the world. 

Nevertheless, in this country, information about Chinese tech is so obscured beneath cultural stereotypes, propoganda, and huge disparity of dialogue that it's very hard to know what's really going on. By speaking to a few experts in breaking Chinese technology, the Data & Society Research Institute reveals a little bit of the actual picture, straight from the source.
