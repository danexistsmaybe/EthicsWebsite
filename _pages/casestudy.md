## Learning Objectives
- Understand the difference between open source, free, and proprietary software
- Know the different commonly used open sources licenses and their histories
- Be able to apply the open source method to software publication
- Apply open source theory to different aspects of an emerging technology, generative AI
- Speculate on the future of open source software in corporate and consumer development
- Apply open source ideas to areas beyond software
